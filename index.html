Quick guide

A short walkthrough of the prototype's key user journeys.

01
＋

Nurse records temperature

Open Nurse station, choose a patient, enter the temperature and save. Repeat readings require a reason and are not counted as the routine daily check.

02
⌕

Doctor completes rounds

Open Doctor rounds. A routine visit can be completed after today's routine temperature is recorded.

03
▤

Admin manages discharge

Review the no-fever day count, verify local approvals, then finalize discharge in the admin workflow.

04
◔

Monitor outcomes

Record a final outcome and review the mortality rate against the 15% benchmark in the assessment brief.

⚠
Prototype limitations

Data is illustrative and browser-local. The app has no real authentication, server persistence, clinical decision support, or production security controls.
